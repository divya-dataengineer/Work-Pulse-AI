# Work IQ Integration

## Overview

WorkPulse AI integrates with Microsoft Work IQ to provide intelligent, context-aware enterprise assistance.

Work IQ serves as the enterprise intelligence layer that understands relationships between people, meetings, emails, chats, documents, and business activities across Microsoft 365.

## How Work IQ Enhances WorkPulse AI

### Enterprise Context Awareness

Work IQ helps WorkPulse AI understand:

- Team interactions
- Meeting discussions
- Email conversations
- Shared documents
- Organizational relationships

### Action Intelligence

Using Work IQ, WorkPulse AI can:

- Identify action items
- Detect task owners
- Track deadlines
- Monitor commitments
- Surface follow-up requirements

### Risk Detection

Work IQ enables WorkPulse AI to identify:

- Delayed deliverables
- Missed commitments
- Unresolved blockers
- Escalation risks

### Productivity Insights

WorkPulse AI provides:

- Meeting summaries
- Decision tracking
- Accountability monitoring
- Recommended next actions

## Expected Outcome

WorkPulse AI transforms enterprise conversations into actionable business outcomes by leveraging Work IQ's contextual intelligence and organizational memory.
