# WorkPulse AI Demo Script

## Introduction

Organizations spend countless hours in meetings, emails, and discussions. However, important action items and decisions often get lost.

## Introducing WorkPulse AI

WorkPulse AI is an Enterprise Action Intelligence Agent built for Microsoft 365 Copilot.

It transforms conversations into accountable actions and measurable business outcomes.

## Scenario 1: Meeting Analysis

User:
"What happened in yesterday's project review meeting?"

WorkPulse AI:
- Generates summary
- Extracts decisions
- Identifies action items
- Highlights risks

## Scenario 2: Action Tracking

User:
"What tasks are currently pending?"

WorkPulse AI:
- Lists pending tasks
- Displays owners
- Shows due dates

## Scenario 3: Risk Detection

User:
"What risks should leadership be aware of?"

WorkPulse AI:
- Identifies blockers
- Detects delays
- Recommends mitigation actions

## Business Impact

WorkPulse AI improves accountability, productivity, and execution across enterprise teams.
