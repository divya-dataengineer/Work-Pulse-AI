# WorkPulse AI

## Microsoft Agent League 2026 – Enterprise Agents Track

WorkPulse AI is an Enterprise Action Intelligence Agent built for Microsoft 365 Copilot. The solution transforms meetings, emails, chats, and business conversations into actionable insights, tracked commitments, identified risks, and measurable business outcomes.

---

## Problem Statement

Enterprise teams spend significant time reviewing meetings, tracking action items, identifying project risks, and preparing status reports.

Important information is often scattered across meetings, emails, and collaboration platforms, leading to missed deadlines, delayed decisions, and reduced productivity.

---

## Solution Overview

WorkPulse AI acts as an intelligent enterprise assistant that analyzes business conversations and automatically generates:

* Meeting Summaries
* Action Item Extraction
* Risk Analysis
* Productivity Insights
* Follow-up Recommendations
* Executive Status Reports

The solution is designed to integrate with Microsoft 365 ecosystems and leverage Microsoft Work IQ capabilities for enterprise intelligence.
